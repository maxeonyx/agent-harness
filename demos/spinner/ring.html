Spinner: one glyph

The node is one shape. What it waits on is its form, how it moves says it is alive, and a change of state is a morph from one form to the next.

The language

Model
An orbit. Sent and waiting for the first byte: a short arc circling, its orbit tightening and quickening the longer it waits. Streaming: the ring closes and a wave flows round it, swelling with each chunk.
Tools
A gear, turning in ticks: mechanical, not fluid. Background work it listens on is the same gear, faint and slower.
Brain
The shape pulls in to a small solid core: the harness thinking to itself. It lasts as long as the brain works, so it is usually a brief morph between the others.
Max
A full maroon disc that breathes once for each keepalive from the brain, about every 4 s when the link is idle. If the breathing stops, the link has.
Starting
A dotted ring, slowly turning: the session is still being put together.
Paused, done
Still: grey for paused, parked or held on the account; green for done.
Data
Any message in or out thickens the rim for a moment, at most every 0.3 s.
Compactions
Pips around the circle from twelve o'clock, the latest brightest, so a chat that collects more of them grows a halo.
