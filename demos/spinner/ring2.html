The language

Waiting for first byte
The rim goes faint and a bold arc orbits it, quickening and pulling in the longer the first byte keeps it waiting. When the stream starts the arc grows round until it closes the ring.
Streaming
Water running round the rim. It reads as flowing, not sliding, for three reasons: the crests are trochoids (sharp crests, flat troughs, as on water, by moving each point along the rim as well as out); the crests ride in groups whose envelope travels at half the crests' speed, so each crest is born at the back of a group, swells, and dies at its front, as deep-water waves do; and a smaller second wave of another length and speed means the pattern never repeats.
Tools
A gear with square teeth that turns in ratchet ticks, with a small mechanical settle after each.
Brain
The gear idea, organic: a smaller cog of five rounded lobes that turns smoothly and slowly. Thinking, not machining. Usually a short morph between the others.
Listening
The tools gear, faint and turning slowly: background work it listens on.
Max
A thick maroon rim on a blush fill that swells once with each keepalive from the brain, about every 4 s when the link is idle. If it stops, the link has.
Starting
The rim breaks into dashes, slowly turning: the session is still being put together.
Paused, done
Still: grey for paused, parked or held on the account; green for done.
Liveness
Each message in or out, keepalives included, drops a disk of the rim's colour behind the node that spreads and fades over about a second. A burst stacks them, darker where they overlap: never debounced.
Compactions
Dots clockwise from twelve o'clock on a track just outside the border, following its shape; the latest is darkest, and a new one pops in.
