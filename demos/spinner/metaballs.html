Spinner: metaballs

One blob. The centre ball is the session; the model, tool calls and Max sit around it. Whatever it waits on grows out of the centre; data shows as soft disks behind it; compactions are dots on a track round its edge.

spinner-metaballs
Every state at map-node size
Session
The centre ball, with the role letter. When the brain itself works (between the others), the centre grows: the brain is the session doing its own step.
Model
The Claude mark, in Claude's colour. Before the first byte the ball swells and eases in time, its mark still and faint; streaming, it is full size, the mark turns, and every chunk sends out a disk.
Tools
A gear, turning in ticks. Background work it listens on is the same gear, smaller, slower and faint.
Max
A microphone: the move is his, and he answers by talking. The blob goes maroon; each keepalive (every 4 s of quiet) sends out a disk, so if the disks stop, the link has.
Starting, paused, held, done
Starting: the satellites bud out of the centre in turn. Paused and done: they merge back into one ball, grey or green. Held by the account: the model ball, red and still.
Data
Packets start disks behind the active ball, expanding and fading: at most one every 0.2 s, so a fast stream shows five a second, overlapping.
Compactions
Dots on the blob's outline, offset a little outside it: the latest brightest at twelve o'clock, then clockwise, equally spaced along the outline and never closer to each other than that spacing.
