# Demos

Each runs with `demos/NAME/run.sh` unless its README says otherwise, and its README (or, without one, its `run.sh`'s top comment) says what to watch. A shell demo starts its brain as a test brain (`node dev/drive.mjs up --brain NAME`), which prints its link, unless what it shows is how a brain starts (`home`, `reboot`, `relaunch`, parts of `cancel`); a real-model demo runs on Sonnet at low effort, and `MODEL=…` (`haiku/low`, `scripted`) picks another. Costs are on Max's subscription.

| Demo | What it shows | Model | Cost, time |
|---|---|---|---|
| brain | an orchestrator, a fork and an engineer build a small repo; the brain is killed -9 mid-command and relaunched, and carries on with the same limbs | scripted (`MODEL=sonnet/low`: real agents) | free, 30 s (Sonnet: a few dozen requests, 2 min) |
| attach | a fork attaches its `bash` call to its report, and the chat receives the real output after the fork's words | Sonnet low | 4 requests, under 1 min |

| browser | a chat hands a browser agent an expense claim in real pages; the tab is passed to Max, who stops it | Sonnet low | about 10 requests, under 1 min |
| carry-joins | a background spawn whose results a `wait` reads alongside `compact_and_continue`: the carry leg joins both members, so the map draws them on solid lines above the chat, with no dashed planned join; leaves the brain up | scripted | free, 20 s |
| cancel | abandoning tasks (with time to tidy, and past a deadline), Ctrl-C once and twice, a worker passed to Max, a systemd restart, and what still runs after each | scripted (`MODEL=sonnet/low`) | free, 3 min |
| chat-engineer | a chat in a workspace directory starts an engineer on the clone there (its `repo`), which commits in its own clone made beside it | Sonnet low | 3 requests, under 1 min |
| meta-tidy | `spawn` says back fields that mean nothing (a misspelt key, a `brief` with only forks, a `branch` on a fork, a `repo` outside the caller's directory) and starts nothing; a `repo` under it works; `wait` on landed work or on nothing returns at once; a stopped engineer's `compact_and_report` ends its tidy-up turn with its report; spawn lists each role's tool sets, and an engineer's schema has no fresh-agent fields; leaves the brain up | scripted | free, 30 s |
| build-paths | the document is never stored and its modules and wasm are stored forever under their build; a reload takes every module from the cache, and after main.js changes on disk every module comes from the new build's path | scripted | free, 40 s |
| chat-spinner | a scripted chat runs a tool, then streams slowly: its log ends in its blob, docked small above the entry box when scrolled up (a tap goes to the bottom); once it waits on Max the blob stays, still, with the mic, and a tap on it starts dictation, the blob pulsing with your voice; its corner is its name and a status dot; leaves the brain up | scripted | free, 40 s |
| chat-loading | a long and a short scripted chat opened on a network slowed to 2.5 s a request, at desktop and phone size: while the context loads the blob waits in the middle of the empty log, grey, its balls circling; once drawn it glides below the last entry in the chat's state; a chat the page already holds draws at once; leaves the brain up | scripted | free, 45 s |
| chat-fresh | a chat in a plain directory hands a fresh `chat` a task: it works in the same directory, no clone; a role it cannot start is refused, naming the roles it can | scripted | free, 20 s |
| chat-turns | a chat with Max ends its turn while its background work runs, waiting on Max with it still running, and its result starts the next turn; Max's words during the chat's `wait` tell it to answer and end its turn; an autonomous fork is told it cannot finish until its background work settles, and to answer Max and `wait` again; leaves the brain up | scripted | free, 40 s |
| chat-moves | retry, a reply's versions, going back, and a retry that gave the same words; leaves the brain up to try edit and back | scripted | free, 30 s |
| composer | the composer: stacked dictate and send with the scrollbar at the box's edge, the one model and effort pill with its menus, styled hints; leaves the brain up | scripted | free, 30 s |

| finished-readonly | Max writes to two forks during their last turns (one ending with a reply, one with `compact_and_report`): both finish there without reading his words, and the orchestrator gets each one's answer as its result, followed by his words verbatim under their label, as written to it after it was done; then words Max writes to a finished fork are held, not heard, continuing one is refused, and continuing the orchestrator's finished chat starts a new one; leaves the brain up to see the read-only line on the page | scripted | free, 40 s |
| compaction | an agent compacts between steps (`compact_and_continue`) and ends with its report (`compact_and_report`); Max's words and the reports are kept word for word; a compaction loop is refused | scripted (`MODEL=sonnet/low`) | free, 1 min (Sonnet: a few dozen requests, 2 min) |
