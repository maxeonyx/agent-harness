# composer

`demos/composer/run.sh`: the composer's details, on the scripted model (free, about 30 s plus the build). It leaves the brain up with one chat whose box holds a long draft.

Watch, in the shots and then in the open page:

- Dictate and send are stacked at the box's right; the words wrap clear of them, and the box's scrollbar is at its own right edge.
- Model and effort are one pill split in two: each half has a pointer, fills its own half on hover, and opens a menu in the page's style (the model's says what changing it rereads). The new chat's role picker opens the same menu.
- Resting the mouse on anything with a hint (the pill, send, the card's close) shows it in the page's style, not the browser's.
- Every close X (the card's, a file viewer's, an attachment's) is centred in its button.
